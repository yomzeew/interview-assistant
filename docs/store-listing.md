**Name:** Interview Caption Assistant

**Short description (132 chars max):**
Live AI interview coach for Google Meet, Teams & Zoom — real-time captions, STAR answers from your CV, speaker detection.

**Full description:**

Interview Caption Assistant is your real-time AI coach for online job interviews. It listens to your interview audio, detects questions, and instantly suggests structured answers drawn from your own CV and preparation notes.

**Key features:**

• **Real-time transcription** — powered by Groq Whisper, captures every word with low latency across Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Zoom

• **Instant AI answers** — every detected question gets a STAR-method answer (Situation → Task → Action → Result) pulled directly from your uploaded CV and interview prep data

• **Speaker diarization** — identifies who is speaking and only triggers AI answers for the interviewer's voice

• **Confidence scoring** — question detection shows a confidence meter so you can tune sensitivity from the Settings panel

• **Answer quality feedback** — rate answers 👍/👎 to teach the AI what works for you. Poor-rated patterns are avoided in future sessions

• **Answer history** — see how you answered the same question in previous sessions and track your improvement

• **Session summary** — when you stop a session, download a full AI-generated post-interview review with gaps identified and practice questions suggested

• **Practice mode** — drill any question with STAR feedback before your interview

• **Multi-language** — transcription in 9 languages, with translation support

• **Pop-out window + Picture-in-Picture** — keep answers visible while you share your browser tab

**Privacy:**
All audio processing happens through your own server endpoint — nothing is sent to third parties except your chosen transcription (Groq/AssemblyAI) and AI (Claude/Groq) providers. No audio is stored permanently.
